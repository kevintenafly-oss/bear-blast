  • Walk with the arrow keys, WASD, the pad, or by tapping a spot. Walking is free.
  • Face a hedge and drop a bomb. Tap a hedge once to aim, tap it again to blast it. Stone walls never break.
  • Every prime-numbered bomb power upgrade (2nd, 3rd, 5th, 7th) also makes the blast reach 1 more square. Longer blasts hit moles and bosses farther away, and the moles' own bombs grow with your power too, up to 3 squares.
  • Tough hedges show dots for their strength. A bomb takes off as many dots as its damage, so upgrade your bomb power in the shop.
  • Orange bomb pickups on the map refill one bomb each, up to your bag size. The bomb bag upgrade goes up to 12.
  • You hold a limited number of bombs. When they run out, each new bomb costs 15 coins. Beat a garden for coins, plus 10 for every bomb you did not use.
  • Garden moles wait along the way to the goal. If one touches you, you lose a heart. From level 4 they throw small bombs, and from level 9 they swing whips. Red or orange marks show where the next hit lands. Walls and hedges block them. Mole bombs and whips hurt other moles too, so lure them into each other.
  • The Easy / Hard button switches the difficulty and restarts the level. Easy: moles move slower, attack less often, have one bomb of health, their bombs stay small and whips stay 2 squares. Hard: moles are faster, take two bombs from level 13, their bombs grow with your bomb power (up to 3 squares) and their whips grow from 2 to 3 to 4 squares as the levels go on.
  • Level 20 has a robot boss. Its bombs blast 4 spaces and it needs 1.2 seconds to wind up every attack, so watch the warning. Its fist flies down a line, costs 2 hearts and makes the floor it crosses vanish for a few seconds.
  • Level 25 is the Mech Ninja. It teleports around the arena, throws 5-space bombs that cost 2 hearts, punches a 3-wide fist for 3 hearts and shakes whole patches of floor away. It has far more health, so bring your best bomb power.
  • Level 10 has a ninja boss guarding the portal. His bombs blast 4 spaces in a line, but stone walls and hedges stop the blast. Hide behind them.
